      Plain-English glossary
      
    
      
      
      Watching (paper mode)
      The program follows real prices and pretends to trade. No money moves. This is how you test it.
      
      
      Live mode
      Real orders with real money. Needs three separate confirmations.
      
      
      Price gap
      The same coin costs slightly different amounts on different exchanges. The bot buys where it is cheap and sells where it is dear, at the same instant.
      
      
      Fees
      Each exchange takes a cut of every trade. A gap only pays if it is bigger than both cuts together. This is the whole game.
      
      
      bps (basis points)
      Hundredths of a percent. 100 bps = 1%. Easier: 1 bp is $1 on every $10,000 you trade.
      
      
      After fees
      What is left of a price gap once both exchanges' fees and a safety margin are subtracted. Positive means a trade would have paid.
      
      
      Opportunity
      A moment when a gap beat the fees by at least your minimum. Rare at normal fee levels.
      
      
      Taker / maker
      A taker trades immediately and pays the higher fee. A maker leaves an offer on the board and pays the lower fee, but only earns if someone takes the offer.
      
      
      Maker mode
      The bot leaves standing offers on the cheap exchange and, when one is taken, instantly does the opposite trade on the other exchange.
      
      
      Hedge
      The opposite trade that locks in the gap, so you are never left holding the coin.
      
      
      Record / Rank
      Record saves every price the bot sees to a file. Rank reads that file and tells you which coins came closest to paying, at your fees.
      
      
      Emergency stop / Pause file
      Two ways to stop orders immediately. The bot keeps watching so you can resume.
      
      
      Agent
      Speedcraft running on another computer (a Linux VM) that you control from this screen.
